Physics and Engineering Constraints
 
The fundamental vacuum equilibrium relation governing the sealed cell is defined by:
P(t) = P0 * e^(-Seff/V * t) + (Qout / Seff) + (∫ Qperm(τ)dτ / V)
 
For a non-pumped, sealed cell (Seff = 0 after initial getter saturation), the rate of pressure rise is directly proportional to the specific outgassing rate q:
dP/dt = (q * A) / V
 
Design Constraint:
For a V = 5 mL cell with surface area A ≈ 15 cm², maintaining a pressure rise of less than 10⁻¹⁰ mbar over 3 years (t ≈ 9.46 × 10⁷ s) mandates a maximum allowable outgassing rate of:
q ≤ 3.5 × 10⁻²¹ mbar·L/(cm²·s)
