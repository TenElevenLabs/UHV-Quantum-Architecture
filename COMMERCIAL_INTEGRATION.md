Commercial Integration Guide: From Open Standard to Field-Ready Systems
 
The Open Standard
 
This repository is maintained by TenElevenLabs, the open-source research and development initiative operating under Mahtan Advanced Defense Systems.
 
We provide the foundational physics, design parameters, and metrology standards for micro-scale Ultra-High Vacuum (µUHV) packages. By open-sourcing this architecture, we are establishing a baseline for low-SWaP (Size, Weight, and Power) quantum systems.
 
The Manufacturing Reality (The Valley of Death)
 
While the physics is open, the manufacturing execution is not trivial. Transitioning from this standard to a field-deployable system requires capabilities that are currently restricted to specialized fabrication environments:
1. High-Temperature Brazing: Creating the Kovar-to-Macor hermetic seals without introducing micro-fissures requires vacuum brazing at controlled ramp rates.
2. Cryogenic Survival: Ensuring the YBCO coils and epoxy potting survive continuous 300 K to 77 K thermal cycling without delamination.
3. Vibration Isolation: Achieving sub-µg residual acceleration requires dual-stage active and passive isolation tuned specifically to the cryocooler mechanical noise.
4. MIL-STD Compliance: Deploying into defense, aerospace, or deep-tech mining environments requires qualification against MIL-STD-810H (shock, vibration, thermal).
